Before you download

Thrive isn't for everyone.

It's a good fit if

  • You want to understand your triggers, not just count days
  • You'd rather do this privately than in a group
  • You've tried quitting on willpower and it didn't hold
  • You want something in your pocket at 2am

Look elsewhere if

  • You're in medical withdrawal — that needs a doctor, not an app
  • You want a website blocker and nothing else
  • You're looking for live human therapy sessions

Thrive is a self-help tool, not a treatment for substance use disorder. If you're in crisis, contact 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline or SAMHSA's treatment locator.
